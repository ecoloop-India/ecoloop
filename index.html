Rules & constitutional basis

19 banned single-use plastic items

Under the national ban in force from 1 July 2022 (Plastic Waste Management Amendment Rules, 2021):

  • Earbuds with plastic sticks
  • Balloon sticks
  • Plastic flags
  • Candy sticks
  • Ice-cream sticks
  • Polystyrene (thermocol) for decoration
  • Plates
  • Cups
  • Glasses
  • Forks
  • Spoons
  • Knives
  • Straws
  • Trays
  • Wrapping films around sweet boxes
  • Invitation cards
  • Cigarette packets
  • PVC banners under 100 micron
  • Stirrers

The 120-micron rule

Plastic carry bags must be at least 120 microns thick (since 31 December 2022, up from 75 microns). Thicker bags are harder to discard after one use and easier to reuse and recycle.

Constitutional hooks

Article 21: the right to life, which courts have read to include a clean and healthy environment.

Article 51A(g): a fundamental duty of every citizen to protect and improve the natural environment and have compassion for living creatures.

Summary for awareness only. Check current notifications from the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change and the Kerala government for exact scope and exemptions.
